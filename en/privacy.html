Privacy Policy and Data Use Notice

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This page explains how AL AZM GLOBAL REAL ESTATE handles information submitted through its property and marketplace enquiry form. Submitting an enquiry does not create a brokerage contract or an obligation to complete a transaction.

1. Information we collect

We may store your name, contact number, country, city, enquiry type, asset category (real estate, shops, cars or motorcycles), approximate budget, preferred contact time, preferred contact language, details you choose to provide and your consent status.

2. How we use information

  • Understand a purchase, sale, rental, lease or partnership enquiry.
  • Contact you to respond and explain possible next steps.
  • Organize and review the enquiry internally and, when necessary, refer it to an authorized employee or property partner.

3. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell customer data. We do not share contact information with a property owner, broker or external partner except to the extent needed to follow up on the enquiry and consistent with your consent and applicable law. Information may be processed by hosting and database providers required to operate the website.

4. Retention and security

We use access controls and a protected database to reduce unauthorized access. Information is retained for as long as needed to follow up on the enquiry and meet operational or legal obligations; we then review whether continued retention is necessary. Do not submit passwords, payment card numbers, identity documents or sensitive financial information through the form.

5. Your choices

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of your information, or withdraw consent to further contact, by contacting the company. We will review and handle the request in accordance with applicable legal requirements and any mandatory record-retention obligations.

6. Contact

For privacy questions or to request a data correction or deletion, contact the company on WhatsApp: +967 711 273 188.

7. Scope

The company aims to coordinate opportunities across multiple markets, and legal requirements may differ by country. This is a general privacy notice and does not replace any local notice or legal obligation required in a particular market. This policy may be updated when our information practices change.
