Listing and Source Verification Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

We aim to present clear information and reduce the risk of misleading listings. This policy explains the checks applied before an opportunity is included in the company catalogue, and what those checks do not mean.

Core principle: We do not publish a listing merely because it was found online. Its source, reuse permission and scope, information and media rights must be reviewed. Unclear cases remain subject to human review.

1. Source and authority checks

  • Record the person or organization that supplied the listing and available source details.
  • Request evidence of the owner's, agent's or broker's authority where appropriate.
  • Do not assume ownership or authority based only on a public post or an unverified message.

2. Permission and reuse rights

  • Record the permission reference, scope and expiry, and check that it covers the intended use.
  • Permission to reuse a property description does not automatically grant rights to reuse its photos or videos; media rights are checked separately.
  • If permission is unknown, expired or insufficient, the listing remains unpublished pending review.

3. Data quality and misleading information

  • Review essential information for completeness and compare it with the available source.
  • Check for duplicates, inconsistencies, unusual details and suspicious information.
  • Do not invent prices, areas, locations or property features; missing information should remain clearly identified.

4. Review and publication

Incomplete, duplicated, suspicious listings or listings without clear permission are held for human review and are not automatically published. Publication is allowed only after required checks and appropriate approvals are documented.

5. What we do not guarantee

Source and data checks are not a legal guarantee of ownership, absence of liens or disputes, accuracy of every statement, continuing availability or price stability. They are not a formal property valuation or a substitute for independent legal and technical due diligence.

Buyers and tenants should independently verify identity, ownership, authority, documents, property condition, price and contract terms, and should not pay any amount before completing appropriate checks.

6. Report an error or request a correction

If you are an owner or authorized representative and believe a published listing is inaccurate or uses content without permission, contact us with the listing URL and evidence of your authority or permission so we can review it.
